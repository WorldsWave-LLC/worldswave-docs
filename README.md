# WorldsWave Documentation

**Official documentation for [WorldsWave](https://worldswave.com) — a free-speech social media, video and live-streaming platform.**

WorldsWave combines a social network, a video platform, live streaming with built-in multistreaming, a forum, a marketplace and creator monetisation in one account. This repository holds the plain-Markdown source of the user documentation, mirrored from the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center).

Every document here is written to answer a specific question directly, so it works as well pasted into an AI assistant as it does read on a page.

## What is WorldsWave?

WorldsWave is a social platform built around unfiltered expression. Its Community Guidelines state that political, religious and moral beliefs, criticism of governments and public figures, and memes, jokes and unpopular opinions are **always protected**, and that the platform does not censor based on viewpoint. What is prohibited is content that is illegal or directly endangers someone — full detail in [Reporting, Blocking and the Rules](docs/reporting-and-rules.md).

**What it includes:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Social** | Posts up to 5,000 characters, 13 attachment types, polls, 12 topic categories |
| **Short video** | **Waves** — a vertical, swipeable feed |
| **Long video** | **Watch** — uploads and live replays |
| **Live streaming** | **WaveDeck** — go live from the browser or OBS, multistream to several platforms at once |
| **Merged chat** | **WaveBot** — chat from eleven platforms in one feed, with an OBS overlay and text-to-speech |
| **Currency** | **Wavium** — NAGs (paid pinned messages) and animated gifts |
| **Creator income** | Monthly subscription tiers, with **0% taken from creators** |
| **Also** | Groups, Pages, Events, Forum, Market, Blog, Jobs, Funding, Games |

**Apps:** Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store.

## Common questions

**Is WorldsWave free?**
Yes. Creating an account, posting, following, messaging and live streaming are all free. Paid plans raise streaming limits — and the top five on the all-time points leaderboard get one for nothing.

**Can I sign up with Google?**
Yes. Google, X (Twitter), Discord and TikTok sign-in are all supported. See [Create an account](docs/create-account-and-profile.md).

**Does WorldsWave take a cut of what creators earn?**
No. WorldsWave takes 0% from creators on subscriptions, NAGs and gifts. The only cut taken anywhere is on the purchase of Wavium, paid by the buyer. See [Wavium, NAGs and Gifts](docs/wavium-nags-and-gifts.md).

**Can I multistream to Twitch and Kick at the same time?**
Yes, that is what WaveDeck is for. The number of simultaneous destinations depends on your plan. See [the WaveDeck guide](docs/wavedeck-guide.md).

**Can I be banned for my political opinions?**
No. See [Reporting, Blocking and the Rules](docs/reporting-and-rules.md).
