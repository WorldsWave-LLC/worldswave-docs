# How to Advertise on WorldsWave: Boosting Posts and Running Ad Campaigns

> Two ways to get seen on WorldsWave: boost your own posts and pages to the community, or run a paid ad campaign with targeting, placements and pay-per-click bidding.

*Also published in the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1019-advertising-and-boosting).*

---

**WorldsWave gives you two separate ways to get in front of more people.** **Boosting** pushes your own posts and pages out to the WorldsWave community, and how many you can boost at once depends on your plan. **Ad campaigns** are paid advertising with targeting, placements, a budget and a bidding model.

Boosting promotes something you already posted. An ad campaign is a new thing you build from scratch, and it can point anywhere — including off the platform.

## At a glance

- **Boost a post:** your own post's **⌄** menu → **Boost Post**
- **See what you have boosted:** worldswave.com/boosted-posts and /boosted-pages
- **Boost limits** depend on your plan, and the page tells you yours
- **Run an ad:** **Create → Create Ad**, or worldswave.com/advertise
- **Manage ads:** worldswave.com/advertise → **Campaigns**
- **Bidding:** **Pay Per Click at $0.225**, or **Pay Per Impression at $0.075**
- **9 placements**, from the whole site down to a single section
- **Targeting:** country and gender
- **Ad creative is an image** in every placement

## What is the difference between boosting and advertising?

**Boosting is about reach inside WorldsWave. Advertising is a campaign you pay to run.**

| | Boosting | Ad campaigns |
|---|---|---|
| **What it promotes** | A post or page you already have | A new ad you build |
| **Where it points** | The post or page itself | Any website URL |
| **Cost model** | Included with your plan, capped by tier | Budget plus per-click or per-impression bidding |
| **Targeting** | None | Country, gender, placement |
| **Where you manage it** | /boosted-posts and /boosted-pages | /advertise |

If you want more people on WorldsWave to see something you posted, boost it. If you are driving traffic somewhere, selling something, or want control over who sees it, run a campaign.

## How do I boost a post?

**Open the ⌄ menu on your own post and choose Boost Post.** That adds it to your boosted list, and it becomes more visible to the WorldsWave community.

Your own posts have a fuller menu than other people's. The full set:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Boost Post** | Adds the post to your boosted list |
| **Pin Post** | Pins it to the top of your profile |
| **Edit Post** | Changes the post's content |
| **Delete Post** | Removes it completely |
| **Disable comments** | Stops members commenting on that post |
| **Mark as Intense Content** | Puts a content warning on it |
| **Copy Link** | Copies its link |
| **Open post in new tab** | Opens it on its own page |
| **Embed** | Gives you code to put the post on another site |

### How many posts can I boost?

**It depends on your plan, and the Boosted Posts page tells you your own limit.** Open **worldswave.com/boosted-posts** and the notice at the top states how many you can have boosted at the same time.

The paid plans raise that number, and the counts for each are listed on the plans page at **worldswave.com/go-pro**.

### How do I boost a page?

**The same way, from the page itself**, and your boosted pages are listed at **worldswave.com/boosted-pages**. The limit works the same as posts and is shown on that page.

## How do I run an ad campaign?

**Go to Create → Create Ad, or worldswave.com/advertise, and press New Campaign.** You fill in the creative, choose who sees it and where, set a budget, and pick how you pay.

The form asks for:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Company name** | Who the ad is from |
| **Campaign title** | The headline |
| **Campaign description** | The body copy |
| **Image** | The creative. Every placement takes an image |
| **Website URL** | Where the ad sends people — on WorldsWave or anywhere else |
| **My Pages** | Optionally attach the ad to one of your WorldsWave pages |
| **Start date** and **End date** | When it runs |
| **Location** | Where you are advertising from |
| **Audience** | Countries to target |
| **Gender** | **All**, **Female** or **Male** |
| **Placement** | Where on WorldsWave the ad appears — see below |
| **Campaign Budget** | What you are prepared to spend |
| **Bidding** | Per click or per impression |

An **Estimated reach** figure updates as you narrow the targeting, so you can see what your choices cost you in audience size before you commit.

### Where can my ad appear?

**Nine placements.** Pick the whole site, or one section where the audience is already in the right frame of mind:

- **Entire Site**
- **Post** — in the feed, between posts
- **Sidebar**
- **Jobs**
- **Forum**
- **Movies**
- **Offer** — the creator subscriptions page
- **Fundings**
- **Story**

Every one takes an **image**. There is no text-only or video ad format.

**Section placements usually beat Entire Site.** An ad on the Jobs page reaches people already thinking about work; the same ad site-wide reaches mostly people who are not.

### How much does a WorldsWave ad cost?

**You choose the model and set the budget.** There are two bidding options:

- **Pay Per Click — $0.225.** You pay when someone clicks.
- **Pay Per Impression — $0.075.** You pay when someone sees it.

Per-impression is cheaper per event, so it suits awareness — getting a name in front of as many people as possible. Per-click costs more each time but you only pay for people who actually came. If you are driving traffic somewhere specific, per-click is usually the honest measure of whether it worked.

Your **campaign budget** caps the total either way.

### How do I pay for ads?

**From your advertising balance**, shown at the top of **worldswave.com/advertise**. Top it up from your wallet — the same page carries a **Wavium Wallet** tab.

Note that this is platform credit, not Wavium. Platform credit pays for ads, plans and subscriptions. Wavium is the currency for gifts and NAGs, and the two are not interchangeable — there is [a guide to the difference](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1013-wavium-nags-and-gifts).

## Where do I manage my campaigns?

**worldswave.com/advertise → Campaigns.** It lists everything you have running, along with your current balance. Before you have created anything it tells you so and points you at **New Campaign**.

## Which should I use?

**Start by boosting.** It is included with your plan, it takes one click from a post you already made, and it costs nothing extra. If your best post is not being seen, boosting is the cheapest fix.

**Move to campaigns when you need something boosting cannot do** — sending people to a URL off WorldsWave, targeting a country, choosing which section of the site you appear in, or spending against a set budget with a measurable cost per click.

And if what you actually want is recurring income rather than reach, neither of these is the tool. That is **[creator subscriptions](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1016-creator-subscriptions)**.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I boost a post on WorldsWave?**
Open the ⌄ menu on your own post and choose Boost Post. It is added to your boosted list straight away.

**How many posts can I boost at once?**
It depends on your plan. The Boosted Posts page states your own limit at the top, and the plans page lists the number each tier gets.

**Does boosting cost extra?**
No. Boosting is included with your plan; your plan sets how many things you can boost at the same time.

**How much does advertising on WorldsWave cost?**
You set the budget. Bidding is either $0.225 per click or $0.075 per impression.

**Can I target my ad?**
Yes — by country, by gender, and by which of the nine placements it appears in.

**Can a WorldsWave ad send people to my own website?**
Yes. The campaign takes any website URL.

**What format does the ad creative need to be?**
An image. All nine placements are image placements.

**Where do I see my ad campaigns?**
worldswave.com/advertise, under Campaigns.

**Do I pay for ads with Wavium?**
No. Ads are paid from your advertising balance, which is platform credit. Wavium is a separate currency used for gifts and NAGs.

**What is the difference between boosting and an ad?**
Boosting makes something you already posted more visible inside WorldsWave. An ad is a campaign you build, target and pay for, and it can point anywhere.
