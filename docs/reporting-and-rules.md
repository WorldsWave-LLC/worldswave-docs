# Reporting, Blocking and the Rules on WorldsWave

> How to report a post or user on WorldsWave, block someone, appeal a decision, and what the Community Guidelines actually prohibit. Free speech first, illegal content never.

*Also published in the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1018-reporting-and-rules).*

---

**WorldsWave protects viewpoints, not crimes.** The Community Guidelines open by saying political, religious and moral beliefs, criticism of governments and public figures, and memes, jokes and unpopular opinions are **always protected** — and that WorldsWave never censors based on viewpoint.

What is not allowed is content that is illegal or directly endangers someone. This guide covers where that line sits, how to report something that crosses it, and what happens next.

## At a glance

- **Report a post:** the **⌄** menu on the post → **Report Post**
- **Report a user:** their profile → **Report**
- **Block someone:** their profile; your list lives at Settings → **Blocked Users**
- **Hide one post without reporting:** post menu → **Hide post**
- **Enforcement:** warning → up to 14-day suspension → permanent ban
- **Some things skip straight to a permanent ban** — violence, threats, targeted hate
- **Appeal:** email **support@worldswave.com**
- **Copyright:** **copyright@worldswave.com**
- **Child safety:** **support@worldswave.com**, prioritised and handled urgently

## What is allowed on WorldsWave?

**Almost everything you think, and nothing that breaks the law.** The Community Guidelines name three categories as always protected:

- **Political, religious and moral beliefs**
- **Criticism of governments and public figures**
- **Memes, jokes and unpopular opinions**

The guidelines are explicit that WorldsWave does not censor based on viewpoint. Disagreeing with someone, finding them offensive, or being annoyed by their politics is not grounds for removing them.

## What is prohibited?

**Four categories, all of them about legality or direct harm** rather than opinion:

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Illegal content** | Terror threats, criminal instructions, exploitation |
| **Direct harm** | Specific threats of violence, or doxxing |
| **Deception** | Scams, phishing, bot-run networks |
| **Copyright** | Sharing content you do not own |

### Are there extra rules for streams, blogs or groups?

**Yes, three sets, one per feature.**

- **Live streaming and video** — no nudity, no sexually explicit content, and no fake emergencies (swatting).
- **Blogs and posts** — no plagiarism and no fake giveaways. Use your own voice.
- **Groups and pages** — admins are responsible for their own community, and impersonation accounts must be labelled as such.

## How do I report a post?

**Open the ⌄ menu at the top right of the post and choose Report Post.** You pick a reason from a dropdown and can add your own explanation in a text box, then send it.

The same menu has other options worth knowing:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Report Post** | Sends the post to the WorldsWave team for review |
| **Hide post** | Removes it from your feed only. Nobody is told, and no report is filed |
| **Save Post** | Adds it to your saved list |
| **Copy Link** | Copies the post's link |
| **Open post in new tab** | Opens it on its own page |
| **Embed** | Gives you code to put the post on another site |

**Use Hide post when something simply is not for you.** Reports are for content that breaks the rules, and a report queue full of "I disagreed with this" makes the real ones slower to reach.

### How do I report a user or a comment?

**A whole account is reported from their profile** — there is a **Report** control there, with the same reason-and-explanation form.

**Comments are reported individually** from the comment itself, and you get a confirmation once it is sent.

## How do I block someone?

**Block them from their profile.** A blocked user cannot interact with you, and your list lives at **Settings → Blocked Users**, where you can unblock anyone at any time.

Blocking and reporting are different jobs and you can do both:

- **Block** — a private wall between you and one person. No review, nothing happens to their account.
- **Report** — asks the WorldsWave team to look at whether something broke the rules.

There are also softer controls in **Settings → Privacy**: you can limit who can follow you, who can message you, and who can post on your timeline, and turn on **Confirm request when someone follows you** so you approve followers yourself. For most unwanted attention, tightening those settings solves the problem without needing anyone blocked.

## What can I do about my own posts?

**Two tools sit in the ⌄ menu on your own posts**, and both are worth knowing before you need them.

- **Disable comments** — stops members commenting on that one post. Useful when a thread turns into something you do not want to moderate.
- **Mark as Intense Content** — puts a content warning on the post, so people choose whether to look. If you are posting something graphic that is within the rules but hard to watch, use this rather than hoping nobody complains.

The same menu also has **Edit Post** and **Delete Post**. Fixing or removing your own post is always faster than arguing about it, and deleting something that broke a rule before it is reported is the simplest way to avoid a strike.

## What happens after I report something?

**A person looks at it.** WorldsWave uses a combination of automated detection and user reports to find violations, but the Community Guidelines describe the enforcement process as hand-reviewed.

Enforcement runs in three steps, unless the violation is severe enough to skip them:

1. **First offence** — the content is removed and a formal warning is issued explaining the violation.
2. **Second offence** — the content is removed and the account is suspended for **up to 14 days**. Posting, commenting and interaction are restricted during that time.
3. **Third offence** — permanent termination. Registration details, IP addresses and identifiers may be blocked from creating future accounts.

### What skips the warnings?

**Three things end an account on the first offence:**

- Content engaging in, encouraging or promoting **unlawful violence or violent extremism**
- **Hate speech or targeted harassment** based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, disability or any other immutable characteristic
- **Direct threats of physical harm** or severe illegal acts

Note the distinction the policy draws, because it is the whole basis of how WorldsWave works: an **opinion about a group** is protected speech, while **targeted harassment of a person** is not. The first is a viewpoint. The second has a victim.

## How do I appeal a decision?

**Email support@worldswave.com.** If your content was removed or your account was actioned and you believe it was a mistake, you can ask for it to be reviewed.

Include the relevant details and context, and be accurate. Appeals are re-evaluated by the WorldsWave team against the original decision, and there are three possible outcomes:

- **Action upheld**
- **Action reversed**
- **Action modified**

Two things worth knowing. **Repeatedly abusing the appeals system can get your ability to appeal limited**, and a decision made after an appeal review is **final**.

If you were caught by automated detection rather than a human report, the appeal route is the same — the Repeat Violator Policy specifically covers content flagged in error by the automated tools.

## How do I report a copyright violation?

**Email copyright@worldswave.com** — not the in-app report button. WorldsWave complies with the DMCA, and a copyright notice needs six things:

1. Your full legal name and contact information
2. A description of the copyrighted work
3. The **URL or URLs** of the infringing content
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised
5. A statement that your information is accurate, under penalty of perjury
6. Your electronic or physical signature

On a valid complaint, WorldsWave reviews the claim, removes or disables access to the content if necessary, and **notifies the uploader so they can file a counter-notice**.

Content ownership on WorldsWave stays with the creator: uploading something does not hand it over. By uploading you are confirming you own it or have permission to share it.

## How do I report something involving a child?

**Email support@worldswave.com, or use the in-app report.** WorldsWave has a **zero-tolerance policy** on child sexual abuse and exploitation, and reports about child safety are **prioritised and handled with urgency**.

Child Sexual Abuse Material is illegal and strictly forbidden. WorldsWave monitors for it, removes it, reports it, and cooperates with law enforcement. There is a designated **Child Safety Officer**, reachable at the same address.

If you come across this kind of content, report it — do not share it, screenshot it, or repost it to draw attention to it.

## How do I report a bug rather than a person?

**The Report A Bug button sits at the top right of every WorldsWave page.** It asks for a short title, a description of what you expected and what happened instead, and the page it happened on.

That is for things that are broken. Content that breaks the rules goes through the report flows above.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I be banned for my political opinions on WorldsWave?**
No. The Community Guidelines state that political, religious and moral beliefs, criticism of governments and public figures, and unpopular opinions are always protected, and that WorldsWave does not censor based on viewpoint.

**What is the difference between blocking and reporting?**
Blocking is private and instant, and stops one person interacting with you. Reporting asks the WorldsWave team to review whether something broke the rules.

**Does the person know I reported them?**
Reports go to the WorldsWave team for review. Hiding a post is entirely private.

**How many warnings do I get?**
Content removal plus a warning on the first offence, a suspension of up to 14 days on the second, and permanent termination on the third — unless the violation is severe enough to skip straight to a ban.

**What gets you banned immediately?**
Promoting unlawful violence or violent extremism, hate speech or targeted harassment based on immutable characteristics, and direct threats of physical harm.

**How long is a suspension?**
Up to 14 days, with posting, commenting and interaction restricted.

**Can I make a new account after a ban?**
No. Registration details, IP addresses and identifiers may be blocked from creating future accounts.

**How do I appeal?**
Email support@worldswave.com with the details. Outcomes are upheld, reversed or modified, and the decision after review is final.

**Who do I contact about copyright?**
copyright@worldswave.com, with the six items a DMCA notice requires.

**Is nudity allowed on WorldsWave?**
No. Nudity and sexually explicit content are prohibited on live streams and video.

**What if I just do not want to see someone's posts?**
Use **Hide post** on a single post, or tighten who can reach you under Settings → Privacy. Neither files a report.
