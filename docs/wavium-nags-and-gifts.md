# Wavium, NAGs and Gifts: Getting Paid on WorldsWave

> How Wavium works on WorldsWave: buy it in your wallet, send NAGs and gifts to creators on stream, and withdraw what you earn. WorldsWave takes 0% from creators.

*Also published in the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1013-wavium-nags-and-gifts).*

---

**WorldsWave takes 0% from creators.** Every NAG and every gift a viewer sends you arrives in your wallet in full, and it is withdrawable. The only cut WorldsWave takes is on the purchase of Wavium itself — the same model TikTok uses for coins.

This guide explains what Wavium is, how NAGs and gifts work, and where your earnings land.

**Money going the other way:** you may not have to pay for a plan at all. The top five on the all-time points leaderboard get **Wave Prime** or **World+** free — see [Points and Leaderboards](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1014-points-and-leaderboards).

## At a glance

- **Wavium (⚡)** — the WorldsWave currency. Buy it, send it, withdraw it
- **Exchange rate** — **$1 = 100 Wavium**, with bonus Wavium on larger packs
- **Bought** — on the website only, not in the mobile apps
- **Creator cut** — **0%.** WorldsWave takes nothing from what you receive
- **Two ways viewers pay you** — **NAGs** (pinned paid messages) and **gifts** (animated, shown on your stream)
- **Both live** in the Live Chat box on your stream page, as **GIFTS** and **NAGS** buttons
- **Where it lands** — your Wavium Wallet at worldswave.com/wallet, withdrawable

## Four things with similar names — which is which?

WorldsWave has four separate balances and it is easy to mix them up. They are not interchangeable.

| | What it is | Can you withdraw it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Wavium** ⚡ | The currency. Earned from NAGs and gifts, spent on live gifts. | **Yes** |
| **Platform credit** | A dollar balance in your wallet. Tops up by card, and is what you buy Wavium with. Also pays for ads, subscriptions and Pro plans. | No |
| **Wavium credits** | A separate thing used only for WaveBot's AI co-host and AI Media Studio. | No |
| **Points** | Activity score on the Earn page. Feeds the leaderboard, which hands out free Pro plans. Not money. | No |

The short version: **platform credit buys Wavium, Wavium is the money, credits are for AI, points are for the leaderboard.**

## What is Wavium?

Wavium is WorldsWave's virtual currency, shown as a ⚡ symbol. Viewers buy it, then spend it sending NAGs and gifts to creators. Creators receive Wavium and can withdraw it.

It works the same way TikTok coins do: money in, spent on-platform, cashed out by whoever receives it.

## How do I buy Wavium?

Go to **worldswave.com/wallet**, use **Add Funds** to top up your platform credit, then choose a Wavium pack. Purchases are **charged from your World's Wave wallet**, not from a card at the point of purchase.

The base rate is **$1 = 100 Wavium**, and larger packs carry a bonus on top:

| Pack | Wavium | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| $5 | 550 ⚡ | +10% |
| $10 | 1,200 ⚡ | +20% |
| $20 | 2,600 ⚡ | +30% |
| $50 | 7,000 ⚡ | +40% |

You can also top up without leaving a stream — both the NAG and gift panels show your Wavium balance with a **Top up** button beside it.

### Can I buy Wavium in the mobile app?

No. **Wavium is bought on the website only.** If you are on a phone, use a browser rather than the app.

## How do viewers pay creators?

Two ways, both paid in Wavium. On a live stream page, both sit at the bottom of the **Live Chat** box as a **GIFTS** button and a **NAGS** button.

Creators switch them on per stream in WaveDeck under **Allow Tips** and **Allow Gifts**.

### NAGs

A NAG is a paid message that gets **pinned for everyone watching** — WorldsWave's take on a superchat. Pay more and it stays pinned longer; the panel tells you how long before you send.

Sending one:

1. Open **NAGS** in the Live Chat box
2. Drag the slider to set how much Wavium to attach. The price and the pin duration update together
3. Choose a tone — **Praise** or **B\*tch**
4. Write your message, up to **280 characters**
5. Check the **preview**, which shows exactly how your NAG will appear
6. **Buy and Send** — if your balance is short, this tops up and sends in one step

### Gifts

A gift is paid in Wavium and **triggers an animation on the streaming page**, so the whole audience sees it land rather than just people reading chat.

Open **GIFTS** in the Live Chat box for the catalogue, each with its Wavium price:

| Gift | Wavium |
|---|---|
| Rose | 1,000 |
| Awesome | 1,000 |
| Heart | 2,000 |
| Star | 3,000 |
| Ice Cream | 5,000 |
| Butterfly | 5,000 |

The panel shows your balance at the top and flags **not enough** if a gift is out of reach, with a **Top up** button beside it.

## Where do my earnings go?

Everything you receive lands in your **Wavium Wallet** at worldswave.com/wallet. The page shows your Wavium balance with its approximate dollar value, and labels it **Withdrawable**.

The wallet holds two different balances side by side:

- **Wavium** — *"Earned from gifts and tips, and what live gifts are spent from. Withdrawable."*
- **Platform credit** — *"tops up below, spends on ads, subscriptions and Pro. Not Wavium, and not withdrawable."*

Below that, a **Transactions** table logs every movement with a type — **SENT**, **RECEIVED** or **Credits** — plus a description, date and amount.

**Send Wavium** lets you send Wavium to another user directly, and **Add Funds** tops up your platform credit.

## Does WorldsWave take a cut of what I earn?

**No. WorldsWave takes 0% from creators.** A NAG or gift sent to you arrives in your wallet in full. The only cut taken anywhere is on the purchase of Wavium, which the viewer pays — the same arrangement TikTok uses for coins.

## What about the Earn page — isn't that where I get paid?

No, and this catches people out. **Dashboard → Earn is about points and the leaderboard, not money.** Your earnings live in the Wallet.

Points are an activity score, and **the top five on the all-time leaderboard get a Pro plan for free** — ranks 1–3 take Wave Prime, ranks 4–5 take World+, checked automatically. Full details in [Points and Leaderboards](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1014-points-and-leaderboards).

Points are covered in their own guide.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does WorldsWave take a percentage of my gifts and NAGs?**
No. WorldsWave takes 0% from creators. Everything a viewer sends reaches you in full. The only cut is taken when Wavium is purchased.

**Can I buy Wavium on my phone?**
Not in the app. Wavium is purchased on the website only. You can use a mobile browser.

**What is the difference between a NAG and a gift?**
A NAG is a paid message pinned in chat for everyone watching, with a tone of Praise or B\*tch and up to 280 characters. A gift triggers an animation on the streaming page.

**How long does a NAG stay pinned?**
It depends on how much Wavium you attach. The slider shows the duration as you move it.

**How much is Wavium worth?**
$1 buys 100 Wavium. Larger packs add a bonus on top, up to +40% on the $50 pack.

**What is the difference between Wavium and Wavium credits?**
Wavium is the currency for gifts, NAGs and purchases on the site. Wavium credits are a separate thing used only for WaveBot's AI co-host and AI Media Studio.

**Is my platform credit the same as Wavium?**
No. Platform credit is a dollar balance that pays for ads, subscriptions and Pro plans, and is what you buy Wavium with. It is not withdrawable. Wavium is.

**Can I send Wavium to another user?**
Yes. There is a **Send Wavium** button in your wallet.

**What if I do not have enough Wavium to send a gift?**
Both panels show a **Top up** button. On a NAG, **Buy and Send** tops up and sends in one step.

**How do I turn gifts and NAGs on for my stream?**
In WaveDeck, switch on **Allow Tips** and **Allow Gifts** before you go live. Both are per-stream options.

**Do points earn me money?**
No. Points feed the leaderboard, and the top five places are granted a free Pro plan. They are not a currency and cannot be withdrawn.

## Quick reference

- **Buy Wavium** — Wallet → Add Funds, then pick a pack *(website only)*
- **Send a NAG** — NAGS button in the Live Chat box
- **Send a gift** — GIFTS button in the Live Chat box
- **See my earnings** — worldswave.com/wallet
- **Send Wavium to someone** — Wallet → Send Wavium
- **Turn on gifts and NAGs** — WaveDeck → Allow Tips / Allow Gifts
- **Top up for ads or a Pro plan** — Wallet → Add Funds (platform credit)
