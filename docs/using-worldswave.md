# How to Use WorldsWave: Posting, Waves, Topics and Every Other Feature

> A tour of WorldsWave once you are signed in: every button in the post box explained, plus Waves, Topics, Watch, Blog, Movies, Forum, Market and the rest.

*Also published in the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1017-using-worldswave).*

---

**WorldsWave is a social network, a video platform, a live-streaming service, a forum and a marketplace in one account.** Once you are signed in, everything is reachable from the left sidebar and the **Create** button at the top.

This guide walks through all of it, starting with the post box — because thirteen buttons sit under that text field and most people never find out what they do.

## At a glance

- **Post box:** opens from **Create → Post**, or the box at the top of your profile
- **Post length:** up to **5,000 characters**
- **Attachments:** 13 options, from photos and polls to products and short video
- **Post privacy:** Everyone, People I Follow, People Follow Me, Only me, or Monetized
- **Topics:** 12 categories your post can be filed under
- **Short video:** **Waves** — vertical, swipeable
- **Long video:** **Watch** — every upload and past live replay
- **Also in there:** Blog, Movies, Forum, Market, Groups, Pages, Events, Games, Jobs, Funding

## How do I make a post on WorldsWave?

**Press Create at the top of any page and choose Post**, or use the box at the top of your own profile. Type into the field marked *"What's going on? #Hashtag.. @Mention.. Link.."*, add anything you want to attach, then press **Share**.

That placeholder is telling you three things work inside the text itself:

- **`#hashtags`** file your post into the trending list
- **`@mentions`** notify another user and link to them
- **Links** expand into a preview card

There is an emoji picker at the top right of the text field, and a live character counter next to the Share button counting down from **5,000**.

### What does each button in the post box do?

**Thirteen attachment buttons sit in three rows under the text field.** You can combine several in one post.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Upload Images** | Attach photos — **PNG, JPEG or GIF**, and you can select **more than one** |
| **Search Music** | Search for a track and attach it, rather than uploading an audio file |
| **Upload Video** | Attach one video. You can also set a **custom thumbnail** instead of the auto-generated frame |
| **GIF** | Opens a **Search GIFs** box and attaches the one you pick |
| **Record voice** | Records a voice clip there and then. Your browser will ask for microphone access the first time |
| **Feelings** | Tags the post with what you are doing — see below |
| **Upload Files** | Attach one file of **any type** — a PDF, a zip, whatever |
| **Sell product** | Turns the post into a **product listing** that also appears in the Market |
| **Color** | Gives a text-only post one of **14 gradient backgrounds** |
| **Location** | Tags the post with a place |
| **Audio Upload** | Attach one **audio file** you upload yourself |
| **Create Poll** | Turns the post into a poll — see below |
| **Upload Waves** | Posts a short vertical video to the **Waves** feed instead of the timeline |

Next to Share there is also a **Live** button, which starts a live broadcast rather than a post.

### What are the five Feelings types?

**Feelings tags a post with an activity, not just a mood.** There are five to choose from, and each takes a value you fill in:

1. **Feeling** — happy, tired, and so on
2. **Traveling to** — a destination
3. **Watching** — a film or show
4. **Playing** — a game
5. **Listening to** — music

### How do I post a poll?

**Press Create Poll, and your post text becomes the question.** Two answer fields appear underneath, marked **Answer 1** and **Answer 2**. There is an **Add answer** button if you want more than two.

Fill in the question in the main text box exactly as you would any post — the poll options are separate fields, so do not put them in the text.

### How do I sell something from a post?

**Press Sell product** and a product form opens. It asks for:

- **Name** and **Price**
- **Description**
- **Category** — a long list covering vehicles, electronics, home and garden, and a large digital-goods section: profile themes, banners, icons, font packs, website templates, UI kits, code snippets, scripts and bots, API tools, beats and instrumentals
- **Type** — **New** or **Used**
- **Location**
- **Currency** — **USD, EUR, JPY, TRY, GBP, RUB, PLN, ILS, BRL** or **INR**
- **Units** — how many you have
- **Photos** — several, PNG, JPEG or GIF

The listing appears both as a post and in the **Market**.

### Who can see my post?

**The dropdown to the left of Share controls it**, and it says **Everyone** by default. The options are:

| Setting | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Everyone** | Anyone, including people who are not signed in |
| **People I Follow** | Only accounts you follow |
| **People Follow Me** | Only your followers |
| **Only me** | Nobody but you |
| **Monetized** | Only people subscribed to one of your paid tiers |

**Monetized** is the one worth knowing about if you sell subscriptions — non-subscribers see a locked preview and a prompt to subscribe. It has [its own guide](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1016-creator-subscriptions).

### What is the Category dropdown for?

**It files your post under one of the 12 WorldsWave topics**, so it shows up on that topic's page as well as in the feed. The topics are:

**Free Speech · Politics · News · Gaming · Technology · Religion · Finance · Music · Sports · Fitness · Entertainment · Travel/IRL**

It is optional, and most people skip it. They should not — a post with a topic gets a second place to be found, and the topic pages are public.

## What are Waves?

**Waves are short vertical videos, swiped through one at a time.** They have their own full-screen feed at **worldswave.com/waves**, separate from the timeline, and their own button in the sidebar.

Down the right-hand side of each Wave there is a rail of controls: follow the creator, save it, like, comment, share, repost, a view counter and a **⋮** menu. Chevrons at the edge move to the next and previous Wave.

To post one, use **Upload Waves** in the post box.

## What is the Watch page?

**Every video posted to WorldsWave, newest first** — uploads from creators across the network, plus replays of past live broadcasts. It sits at **worldswave.com/watch**.

Watch also lets you browse by topic, so you can look at just the Gaming or just the Politics videos rather than one mixed feed.

Waves and Watch are the two halves of video on WorldsWave: **Waves is short and vertical, Watch is long and horizontal.**

## What are Topics?

**Twelve permanent public pages, one per subject**, collecting the posts and live streams members have filed under them. **worldswave.com/topics** lists them all with a live count of how much is in each.

Each topic card shows how many posts it holds and how many streams are running in it right now, and a topic marked **live** has someone broadcasting in it at that moment. Gaming additionally counts games.

Filing your own posts under a topic is what the Category dropdown does. Browsing other people's is how you find accounts worth following without relying on an algorithm.

## What is the Blog?

**Long-form articles written by WorldsWave members**, at **worldswave.com/blogs**, sorted with the most recent first. This is where posts that are too long for a timeline entry go — opinion, commentary, creator updates.

Write one from **Create → Create Blog**. Your own are listed under **My articles**. Publishing a blog is also the single biggest points earner on the platform, at **20 points**.

## What are Movies?

**A library of full-length films and documentaries** at **worldswave.com/movies**, sorted by **New**, **Recommended** or **Most watched**. Each carries a genre label and a rating.

This is a curated library rather than a place you upload to — your own videos go to **Watch**.

## What is the Forum?

**Threaded discussion boards**, at **worldswave.com/forum**, for conversations that would get lost in a feed. Each board shows its post and topic counts, and each thread its author and age.

The forum nav has five entries: **Browse Forum**, **Members**, **Search**, **My Threads** and **My Messages**, plus a **Jump to** selector for moving between boards.

## What is the Market?

**A classifieds marketplace** at **worldswave.com/products** — *"Buy and Sell products easily at WorldsWave."*

You can sort and filter by **Categories**, **Location distance** and **Nearby Shops**, and **My Products** holds your own listings. Anything you post with **Sell product** lands here.

## What else is in the sidebar?

Everything above has its own entry, and so does all of this:

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| **News Feed** | Posts from the people you follow — your home |
| **LIVE NOW** | Everyone currently broadcasting |
| **Albums** | Photo albums you create and fill |
| **Saved Posts** | Anything you bookmarked |
| **Popular Posts** | The most-liked posts on the platform |
| **Memories** | Your own posts from this day in previous years |
| **Pokes** | The old-school poke |
| **My Groups** | Groups you have joined or created |
| **My Pages** | Pages you run — brands, shows, projects |
| **Directory** | The Discover page: posts, streams, topics, users and pages in one browser |
| **Events** | Events you can attend or host |
| **Games** | Browser games, with a live count of who is playing |
| **Jobs** | Job listings, filterable by type, category and distance |
| **Offers** | Creators selling subscription tiers |
| **Find friends** | People near you |
| **Common Things** | People who share interests with you, ranked by how many |
| **Funding** | Fundraisers, each showing how much has been raised of its goal |

## What is on the Create menu?

**Seven things, and Post is only the first.** The Create button at the top of every page opens:

1. **Post** — the post box
2. **Create album** — a photo album
3. **Create Ad** — a paid promotion
4. **Create Blog** — a long-form article
5. **Create Event** — an event with a date and place
6. **Create Group** — a community others can join
7. **Create Page** — a page for a brand, show or project

## Frequently asked questions

**How long can a WorldsWave post be?**
Up to 5,000 characters. A counter next to the Share button counts down as you type.

**Can I post more than one photo at once?**
Yes. Upload Images accepts several files, in PNG, JPEG or GIF.

**How do I post a short video like a reel?**
Use **Upload Waves** in the post box. It goes to the Waves feed rather than your timeline.

**What is the difference between Waves and Watch?**
Waves are short vertical videos you swipe through. Watch is every long video on the platform, including replays of live streams.

**How do I make a poll?**
Press Create Poll. Your post text is the question, and you fill in Answer 1 and Answer 2, with Add answer for more.

**Can I change the thumbnail on a video post?**
Yes. Upload Video lets you set a custom thumbnail instead of the automatic one.

**What does the Category dropdown do?**
It files your post under one of the 12 topics, so it appears on that topic's public page as well as in the feed.

**How do I post something only my subscribers can read?**
Set the privacy dropdown to **Monetized**. Non-subscribers get a locked preview.

**Can I sell something on WorldsWave?**
Yes, two ways: **Sell product** in the post box for one-off items, which also lists them in the Market, or subscription tiers for recurring income.

**Where do my uploaded videos appear?**
On your profile's Videos tab and on the Watch page. Waves appear on your Waves tab and in the Waves feed.

**What is Common Things?**
A page that finds members who share interests with you and ranks them by how many you have in common.
