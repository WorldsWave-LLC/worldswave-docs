# How to Create a WorldsWave Account and Set Up Your Profile

> Sign up for WorldsWave with email, phone or Google, verify your account, and set up your profile picture, cover photo, bio and privacy settings. Free, under a minute.

*Also published in the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1015-create-account-and-profile).*

---

**Creating a WorldsWave account is free and takes under a minute.** You need a username, an email address or phone number, and a password — or you can sign in with Google, X, Discord or TikTok and skip the form entirely.

WorldsWave is a free-speech social platform: posts, videos, live streaming, groups, pages and a marketplace, with no hidden algorithm deciding what you see.

## At a glance

- **Sign up at:** worldswave.com/register
- **Log in at:** worldswave.com/welcome
- **Cost:** free
- **You need:** a username, an email **or** phone number, and a password
- **Faster option:** sign in with **Google**, **X (Twitter)**, **Discord** or **TikTok**
- **Verification:** a confirmation code sent by email or SMS — the account is locked until you enter it
- **Apps:** Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store
- **Browsing without an account:** allowed, but you cannot post, follow, message or go live

## How do I create a WorldsWave account?

Go to **worldswave.com/register** and fill in the form. You need a username, an email address, a password typed twice, and a gender. Tick the reCAPTCHA and the terms box, then press **Let's Go!** You will get a confirmation message and an email within a minute or two.

The full field list:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Username** | Your permanent handle and profile URL — `worldswave.com/yourname` |
| **E-mail address** | Where your confirmation code is sent |
| **How would you like to verify your account?** | **E-mail address** or **phone number** |
| **Password** and **Confirm Password** | Must match |
| **Gender** | Shown on your profile Info panel; you can change it later |
| **reCAPTCHA** | "I'm not a robot" |
| **Terms checkbox** | Agrees to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy |

Pick your username carefully — it is your profile address and the name people @mention. Brand and organization names are covered by the WorldsWave Brand & Organization Username Policy, linked in the site footer.

### Can I sign up with Google?

**Yes.** The login page carries a **Sign in with Google** button, plus icons for **X (Twitter)**, **Discord** and **TikTok**. Using one of these creates your WorldsWave account from that account, so there is no form to fill in, no password to remember, and no confirmation code to wait for.

Go to **worldswave.com/welcome** and pick the provider under **Or login with**. The same buttons sign you in again afterwards — use the same provider every time, or you will end up with a second, separate account.

### How do I verify my account?

**Check your inbox for the confirmation email and follow it.** The success message tells you exactly this: *"Registration successful! We have sent you an email, Please check your inbox/spam to verify your email."* Until you confirm, the account exists but is not activated.

If you try to use an unactivated account you get a screen headed **"Oops, looks like your account is not activated yet."** It offers two ways in:

1. Enter your **e-mail address**, or
2. Enter your **phone number** in international format (for example `+1...`)

Then press **Send Confirmation Code** and enter the code you receive.

### What if the confirmation email never arrives?

**Check your spam or junk folder first** — the site's own message tells you to, because confirmation emails from a new sender are commonly filtered. If it is not there, go back to the activation screen and request the code again, or switch to the phone option and have it sent by SMS instead.

Accounts signed up through Google, X, Discord or TikTok skip this step entirely, because the provider has already verified you.

## How do I log in to WorldsWave?

Go to **worldswave.com/welcome**, enter your username and password, and press **Login**. **Remember this device** is ticked by default, so you stay signed in on that browser. **Forgot Password?** sits next to it if you need a reset link.

The same page carries the four social sign-in options. If you created your account with one of them, use that button rather than the username and password fields.

### Is there a WorldsWave app?

**Yes — on Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store.** All three are linked from the bottom of the login page. Your account is the same everywhere, so you can sign up on the website and log straight in on your phone.

One thing that is website-only: **buying Wavium**, the WorldsWave currency. Everything else works on mobile.

## Do I need an account to browse WorldsWave?

**No.** WorldsWave is readable as a guest. Without signing in you can see the posts feed, who is live, Topics, Users, Pages, Groups, Blog, Market, Events, Games, Forum, Movies, Jobs and Fundings, plus the Trending hashtags and Top Users panels.

What you cannot do as a guest is post, comment, react, follow, message, go live, join a group, buy anything, or appear on the leaderboards. The homepage shows a **Create Account** button for exactly that reason.

## What is the Profile Completion checklist?

**A row of buttons on your own profile that walks a new account through setup.** It appears above your timeline as soon as you register and disappears as you fill things in.

The five prompts are:

- **Add your profile picture**
- **Add your name**
- **Add your workplace**
- **Add your country**
- **Add your address**

Before you do any of it, your profile shows your username as your display name and a default avatar. Adding a picture and a real name is the single biggest difference between an account people follow and one they scroll past.

## How do I edit my WorldsWave profile?

**Press Edit on your own profile, or go to Settings → Profile.** That page holds everything that shows in the **Info** panel beside your timeline.

The fields are:

| Field | Shows as |
|---|---|
| **First name** / **Last name** | Your display name, above your username |
| **About me** | Your bio |
| **Location** and **Country** | Where you are |
| **Working at** | "Working at …" in the Info panel |
| **School** | "Studying at …" in the Info panel |
| **Website** | A clickable link in the Info panel |
| **Relationship Status** | Single, and so on |
| **Categories**, **Hobbies**, **Favorite Song**, **Travel**, **Games** | Extra interest fields |

Your **username**, **email**, **phone**, **birthday**, **gender**, **language** and **weather unit** live one page over, under **Settings → General**.

## How do I change my profile picture and cover photo?

**Go to Settings → Avatar & Cover.** WorldsWave states the exact sizes it wants, and using them is the difference between a sharp profile and a blurry cropped one:

- **Profile picture — 400 × 400 px**, square, JPG or PNG. It is **cropped to a circle**, so keep your face centred.
- **Cover photo — 1920 × 480 px**, JPG or PNG, a **4:1 ratio**. Use at least 1200 px wide, and keep the important part centred: **the top and bottom edges crop on some screens.**

Upload both, then press **Save**.

### Can I customise how my profile looks?

**Yes, under Settings → Design.** Two options sit there:

- **Background** — an image pinned full-screen behind your profile. Recommended **1920 × 1080 px**; anything smaller tiles instead of filling the page.
- **CSS file** — upload your own stylesheet to restyle your profile.

The CSS option is for people who know CSS. The background image alone changes the feel of a profile and takes ten seconds.

## How do I add my social links?

**Settings → Social Links.** WorldsWave lists **seventeen** platforms, and any you fill in appear on your profile:

Facebook · X (Twitter) · Instagram · Snapchat · LinkedIn · TikTok · YouTube · Twitch · Kick · Rumble · Odysee · Discord · Gettr · Gab · Parler · Blaze · Streemrz

If you stream, fill in Twitch, Kick, YouTube and Rumble — people who find you on WorldsWave will follow you on the platforms they already use, and it costs you nothing.

## Who can see my profile?

**You control it under Settings → Privacy**, one dropdown per question. The defaults are open, which suits most people on a public platform, but every one of them can be tightened.

The controls are:

- **Who can follow me?**
- **Who can message me?**
- **Who can see my friends?**
- **Who can post on my timeline?**
- **Who can see my birthday?**
- **Confirm request when someone follows you?** — turn this on to approve followers manually
- **Send users a notification when I visit their profile?** — note the warning: turn it off and you lose the ability to see who visited yours
- **Show my last seen?** — same trade: hide yours and you cannot see anyone else's

## What else is in Settings?

Nineteen pages, reached from the gear icon or **worldswave.com/setting**. The ones a new account actually needs:

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **General** | Username, email, phone, birthday, country, gender, language |
| **Profile** | Name, bio and all the Info-panel fields |
| **Avatar & Cover** | Profile picture and cover photo |
| **Design** | Background image and custom CSS |
| **Social Links** | Your seventeen off-platform profiles |
| **Privacy** | Who can follow, message and see what |
| **Password** | Change your password |
| **Two-factor authentication** | Add a second step at login |
| **Manage Sessions** | See and end logins on other devices |
| **Blocked Users** | Your block list |
| **Notification Settings** | What you get told about |
| **My Points** | Your points and leaderboard rank |
| **Wavium Wallet** | Your currency balance and earnings |
| **Monetization**, **My Earnings**, **My Affiliates** | Creator payouts |
| **My Information**, **My Addresses** | Personal and shipping details |
| **Delete Account** | Permanent |

**Turn on Two-factor authentication and check Manage Sessions** before you do anything else. They are the two that matter if your password ever leaks.

## What is on my profile page?

**Nine tabs across the top, and an Info panel down the side.** The tabs are **Timeline, LIVE, Waves, Groups, Likes, Following, Followers, Photos** and **Videos** — each one filters your profile to that kind of content.

Next to **Edit** sits **Activities**, a log of what you have been doing. The **Info** panel on the right shows your online status, post count, website, gender, birthday, relationship status, workplace and school — all of it fed from the settings pages above.

Your timeline also has its own topic filter, so visitors can look at just your Gaming posts or just your Politics posts.

## What should I do first?

In order, and the whole thing takes about five minutes:

1. **Register** at worldswave.com/register, or sign in with Google.
2. **Confirm** your email or phone.
3. **Add a profile picture** at 400 × 400 and a cover at 1920 × 480.
4. **Fill in your name and bio** under Settings → Profile.
5. **Add your social links** so people can find you elsewhere.
6. **Turn on two-factor authentication.**
7. **Follow some people** — use Users, Topics or the Trending panel on the homepage.
8. **Make your first post.**

## Frequently asked questions

**Is WorldsWave free?**
Yes. Creating an account, posting, following, messaging and live streaming are all free. Paid plans (World+ and Wave Prime) raise streaming limits, and the top five on the all-time points leaderboard get one for nothing.

**Can I sign in with Google?**
Yes — Google, X (Twitter), Discord and TikTok are all on the login page under "Or login with".

**Can I change my username later?**
Your username is your profile address, so treat it as permanent when you pick it. It appears under Settings → General.

**What size should my WorldsWave profile picture be?**
400 × 400 px, square, JPG or PNG. It is cropped to a circle.

**What size should my cover photo be?**
1920 × 480 px, a 4:1 ratio, at least 1200 px wide. Keep the important part centred because the top and bottom crop on some screens.

**My account says it is not activated. What now?**
Go to the activation screen, enter your email address or phone number, press Send Confirmation Code, and enter the code. Check your spam folder for the original email first.

**Can I use WorldsWave without an account?**
You can read and watch, but not post, comment, follow, message or go live.

**Is there a mobile app?**
Yes, on Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store. Buying Wavium is the only thing that is website-only.

**How do I stop people messaging me?**
Settings → Privacy → "Who can message me?".

**How do I delete my WorldsWave account?**
Settings → Delete Account. It is permanent.
