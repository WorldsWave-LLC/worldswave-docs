# How to Sell Subscriptions on WorldsWave: Tiers, Monetized Posts and Payouts

> Set up paid subscription tiers on WorldsWave, post subscriber-only content, and withdraw your earnings. WorldsWave takes 0% — you keep the whole subscription.

*Also published in the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1016-creator-subscriptions).*

---

**WorldsWave lets you charge a monthly subscription for access to you.** You set the tiers, you set the prices, and you keep **100%** of what subscribers pay — WorldsWave takes no cut from creators.

A subscription tier bundles two things: **posts you mark as monetized**, which only subscribers can read, and optional direct benefits like being able to message you or watch your subscriber-only streams.

## At a glance

- **Set it up:** Settings → **Monetization**, then **Add New**
- **Billing:** monthly, per tier
- **Platform cut:** **0%** — you keep the full subscription
- **Number of tiers:** as many as you like, each with its own price and benefits
- **Benefits you can attach:** **Chat** (four levels, up to audio and video calls) and **Live Stream**
- **Subscriber-only posts:** set a post's privacy to **Monetized**
- **Where people find you:** worldswave.com/offers, and the **Offers** entry on your profile
- **Getting paid:** Settings → **My Earnings** → PayPal or Stripe, **$50 minimum** withdrawal

## What are WorldsWave subscription tiers?

**A tier is a paid monthly membership to your content.** You give it a name, a price, a description and an image, and pick which benefits come with it. Anyone who subscribes gets everything that tier includes for as long as they keep paying.

This is separate from every other way of earning on WorldsWave. NAGs and gifts are one-off payments a viewer sends during a stream. A subscription is recurring, and it unlocks things rather than just sending money.

It is also separate from **World+** and **Wave Prime**, which are plans *you* buy from WorldsWave. Your subscription tiers are things *your audience* buys from *you*.

## How do I start selling subscriptions?

**Go to Settings → Monetization and press Add New.** The page opens with a single prompt — *"Unlock your earning potential today by offering and selling your exclusive content and posts"* — and one button. Fill in the tier form and save.

The form has five parts:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tier title** | What the tier is called. Subscribers see this. |
| **Tier price** | The monthly amount in dollars. |
| **Benefits** | Tick **Chat**, **Live Stream**, or both. See below. |
| **Tier description** | What subscribers get. This is your sales pitch — write it properly. |
| **Tier image** | The picture shown on the tier card. |

Repeat for each tier you want. A common shape is a cheap tier that only unlocks monetized posts, and a dearer one that adds direct messaging.

### What benefits can I attach to a tier?

**Two, and the first has four levels.** They are direct access to you, on top of whatever monetized posts you publish.

**Chat** — lets subscribers message you. When you tick it, you choose how far that access goes:

1. **Chat without audio and video call** — text messages only
2. **Chat with audio call and without video call** — text and voice
3. **Chat without audio call and with video call** — text and video
4. **Chat with audio and video call** — everything

**Live Stream** — gives subscribers access to your subscriber-only streams.

Every tier also includes your monetized posts. You do not tick a box for that; it is what the Monetized post setting does.

### What should I charge?

**That is yours to decide, and nothing on WorldsWave caps it.** For a reference point, WorldsWave's own plans run $5 to $25 a month, so subscribers are used to seeing prices in that range.

Because the platform takes nothing, a $5 tier is $5 to you, less whatever PayPal or Stripe charge to move it. Price low enough that people do not have to think about it, and let the tier description carry the work.

## What is a monetized post?

**A post only your subscribers can read.** In the post composer, open the privacy dropdown — the one that normally says **Everyone** — and choose **Monetized**. The post then goes out to your subscribers instead of to everyone.

People who are not subscribed still see the post in the feed, but the content is **locked**. They get a preview and a prompt to subscribe rather than the post itself. That is deliberate: a monetized post advertises itself, so it works as a reason to subscribe rather than disappearing.

Use it for the things people are actually paying for — the full version of something you teased publicly, early access, behind-the-scenes, subscriber-only announcements. A feed of nothing but locked posts gives a visitor no reason to follow you in the first place, so keep posting publicly too.

## Where do people find my tiers?

**Two places.** There is a platform-wide **Offers** page at **worldswave.com/offers** that lists creators with tiers available, and your own profile carries an **Offers** entry so anyone looking at you can see what you sell.

A locked monetized post is the third route, and in practice the strongest one — someone who wants to read *that specific post* is already most of the way to subscribing.

## How do I get paid?

**Settings → My Earnings.** It shows your earnings balance, your available funds, and a withdrawal form.

- **Withdraw method:** **PayPal** or **Stripe**
- **Minimum withdrawal:** **$50**
- **Payment History** logs every request with its amount, date and status

One rule the page states plainly, and it catches people out: **you can only withdraw earnings, not wallet top-ups.** Money you put *into* WorldsWave to spend on the platform is not withdrawable. Money your audience pays *you* is.

### Does WorldsWave take a cut of my subscriptions?

**No. WorldsWave takes 0% from creators.** The full subscription price is yours, exactly as with NAGs and gifts. The only cut WorldsWave takes anywhere is on the purchase of Wavium, and that is paid by the buyer.

Your payment processor is separate. PayPal and Stripe each charge their own fee to move money to you; that is between you and them, and it is not a WorldsWave charge.

## How do subscribers manage a subscription?

**At worldswave.com/subscriptions**, which lists everyone they currently subscribe to. Cancelling is an **Unsubscribe** button on the creator's profile.

Worth knowing as a creator: someone who unsubscribes loses access to your monetized posts and to whatever chat or stream benefits the tier carried.

## Why does "Manage Subscription" take me to the plans page?

**Because it means a different kind of subscription.** The **Manage Subscription** link in your own account menu goes to **worldswave.com/go-pro**, which is where you buy **World+** or **Wave Prime** from WorldsWave.

The two are easy to mix up, so to be exact:

| Thing | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Manage Subscription** | The plan you buy from WorldsWave | /go-pro |
| **Subscriptions** | Creators you pay | /subscriptions |
| **Monetization** | Tiers you sell | Settings → Monetization |
| **My Earnings** | What you are owed, and withdrawing it | Settings → My Earnings |

## What are the other ways to earn on WorldsWave?

Subscriptions are one of five, and they stack — nothing stops you running all of them at once.

| How | What it is | Recurring? |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscriptions** | Monthly tiers your audience buys | Yes |
| **NAGs** | Paid messages pinned in your stream chat | No |
| **Gifts** | Wavium gifts that animate on your stream | No |
| **Affiliates** | Up to **10%** when someone you referred buys a WorldsWave plan | Per referral |
| **Points** | Not money — but the all-time top five get a plan free | n/a |

**My Affiliates** sits in Settings and gives you a referral link plus share buttons. It pays out on WorldsWave plan purchases, not on your own subscriptions.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does WorldsWave take from my subscriptions?**
Nothing. WorldsWave takes 0% from creators. Only your payment processor's own fee applies.

**How often are subscribers billed?**
Monthly, at the price set on the tier.

**Can I have more than one subscription tier?**
Yes. Add as many as you like, each with its own title, price, description, image and benefits.

**What can subscribers get besides posts?**
Chat access — at one of four levels, from text-only up to audio and video calls — and access to your subscriber-only live streams.

**What do non-subscribers see when I post a monetized post?**
A locked preview with a prompt to subscribe. The post is visible in the feed but the content is not.

**How do I make a post subscribers-only?**
In the composer, change the privacy dropdown from Everyone to **Monetized**.

**How do I withdraw my earnings?**
Settings → My Earnings, pick PayPal or Stripe, enter an amount and request the withdrawal. The minimum is **$50**.

**Why can't I withdraw my whole balance?**
Only earnings are withdrawable. Anything you topped up yourself to spend on the platform is not.

**Is a subscription the same as Wave Prime?**
No. Wave Prime is a plan you buy from WorldsWave. A subscription is something your audience buys from you.

**Where do people browse creators to subscribe to?**
worldswave.com/offers, and the Offers entry on each creator's profile.
