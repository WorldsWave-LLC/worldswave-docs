# Points and Leaderboards: How to Win a Free Pro Plan on WorldsWave

> How WorldsWave points work, how to climb the rankings, and how the top five on the all-time leaderboard get a Wave Prime or World+ plan for free.

*Also published in the [WorldsWave Help Center](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1014-points-and-leaderboards).*

---

**WorldsWave gives points for everything you do on the platform, and the top five on the all-time leaderboard get a Pro plan for free.** Ranks 1–3 take **Wave Prime**, ranks 4–5 take **World+**. It is checked automatically and can change at any time, so holding your place matters as much as reaching it.

Points are not money. They are an activity score that decides where you sit on the rankings.

## At a glance

- **Where to see the rankings:** worldswave.com/rankings
- **Where to see your own:** Dashboard → Earn
- **Top 100** users listed, filterable by **All Time, Today, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly**
- **Six leaderboards:** Points, Views, Followers, Likes, Stream Views and Hours Streamed
- **The free plan** is decided on the **all-time Points** board only
- **Biggest earner:** publishing a blog, at 20 points
- **Best for streamers:** 15 points for every hour you stream live

## How do I earn points?

Every action on WorldsWave is worth a fixed number of points, and they all feed the same all-time total the leaderboard sorts on.

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Publish a blog | **20** |
| Every hour you stream live | **15** |
| Create a post | **10** |
| Comment on a post | **2** |
| React to a post | **1** |

Streaming is the steadiest way to climb — a four-hour stream is worth 60 points, the same as six posts.

## How do I get a free Pro plan?

Finish in the **top five of the all-time Points leaderboard**.

- **Ranks 1, 2 and 3** — a free **Wave Prime** plan
- **Ranks 4 and 5** — a free **World+** plan

On the rankings page the cut-offs are drawn right into the list, so you can see exactly where the bands sit and how far away you are.

**It is checked automatically and can change at any time.** If someone overtakes you, the plan follows the rank rather than the person, so staying in the band is what keeps it.

### Does this apply to the other leaderboards?

No. The free plan is awarded on the **all-time Points** board only. The Views, Followers, Likes, Stream Views and Hours Streamed boards are for bragging rights — and the weekly, monthly and yearly point views do not carry the bands either.

### What if I am an admin?

Admins hold their leaderboard slot but the automatic grant skips them, so no plan is applied either way. The rankings page says so on your own row.

## What is on the rankings page?

**worldswave.com/rankings** lists the **top 100 users** and is public.

Every row shows the rank, the user's avatar and name, and their score. The free-plan bands appear as dividers in the list.

### Filtering by time

A dropdown switches between **All Time, Today, Weekly, Monthly** and **Yearly**. Shorter windows are a much more level playing field — someone who joined last week can top the weekly board without ever appearing in the all-time top 100.

### The six leaderboards

Buttons along the top switch between them, and each is its own page:

| Leaderboard | What it ranks |
|---|---|
| **Points** | Your all-round activity score. The one that decides the free plans. |
| **Views** | Views on your content |
| **Followers** | Follower count |
| **Likes** | Likes received |
| **Stream Views** | Views on your live streams |
| **Hours Streamed** | Time spent live, shown as hours and minutes |

The other boards list only users who have something to rank, so they are usually shorter than 100 — the Hours Streamed board, for instance, shows just the users who have actually streamed.

## Where do I see my own points?

Go to **Dashboard → Earn**. It shows:

- **Points Today**, **Points This Week**, **Points This Month** and **Total Points**
- Your current **Leaderboard Rank**, and which band you are in
- A chart of your points by day over **7, 30 or 90 days**, with a **Table** view, **Save graph**, **CSV** export and **Open in new tab**
- Your **Wavium** balance, sitting alongside

Click any measurement tile to add it to the chart, and click it again to take it off.

### Why is my chart emptier than my total?

**Per-day point tracking began on 7 August 2026.** Anything you earned before that counts toward your Total Points but has no day-by-day history, so those days are left blank rather than shown as zero. Your Total Points will be higher than the charted history adds up to, and that is expected.

## Do points earn me money?

**No.** Points are an activity score. They cannot be spent, sent or withdrawn — the only thing they get you is leaderboard position and, in the top five, a free plan.

Money on WorldsWave is **Wavium**, which is a different thing entirely and has its own guide: [Wavium, NAGs and Gifts](https://worldswave.help.center/article/1013-wavium-nags-and-gifts). The page called **Earn** is about points; your actual earnings live in your **Wallet**.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I get a free Wave Prime plan?**
Reach the top three of the all-time Points leaderboard. Ranks 4 and 5 get World+ instead.

**What is the fastest way to earn points?**
Publishing a blog is the single biggest action at 20 points. For regular activity, streaming is the strongest — 15 points an hour adds up faster than posting.

**Do I keep the free plan if I drop out of the top five?**
The grant is checked automatically and can change at any time, so it follows the rank.

**Do the weekly and monthly boards give out free plans?**
No. Only the all-time Points board carries the bands.

**Why is the Hours Streamed leaderboard shorter than 100?**
The other boards only list users who have something to rank. If only a couple of dozen people have streamed, that is how many appear.

**Can I lose points?**
Points are an all-time total that actions add to. The day-by-day chart can show a negative movement between days, but Total Points is your lifetime balance.

**Are points the same as Wavium?**
No. Points are a score and cannot be withdrawn. Wavium is the WorldsWave currency, used for NAGs, gifts and purchases, and it is withdrawable.

**Is the leaderboard public?**
Yes. The top 100 is visible to anyone at worldswave.com/rankings.

**Why does my rank say I get no plan even though I am in the top five?**
Admin accounts hold their slot but are skipped by the automatic grant.
